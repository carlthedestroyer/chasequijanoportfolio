Philadelphia Post: STEM Day

February 21, 2026 11:00 am – February 21, 2026 2:00 pm Science History Institute
Register

Science History Institute

315 Chestnut Street,
Philadelphia, PA 19106

Upcoming SAME Events

We are pleased to announce the Society of American Military Engineers (SAME) Philadelphia Post STEM Event on Saturday, February 21, 2025, from 11-2pm at The Science History Institute in Philadelphia.

This event and admission to the museum are FREE. Make a day of it with your family! No cost lunch included! Children of all ages are welcome.

This premier STEM event brings together all school students from the Greater Philadelphia area with an interest in learning about STEM careers in the Architectural, Engineering, and Construction (A/E/C) industry.

SAME Philadelphia Post is leading efforts to inspire, encourage, and enable youth to pursue STEM careers and help develop interest in science and technology.

Become a sponsor or an exhibitor!

This event is a tremendous opportunity for you to inspire future STEM professionals. Being a STEM event exhibitor is an exciting way for your organization to showcase and highlight projects or share your group’s STEM outreach initiatives. Being a sponsor will ensure that we can keep this event free for families.
