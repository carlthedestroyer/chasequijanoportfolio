Problem

Barriers to Affordable Remote Monitoring

Berta Medical was co-founded with Billy Zhang to address barriers in accessing affordable healthcare and remote patient monitoring. Existing wearable ECG solutions were either prohibitively expensive or locked into proprietary ecosystems without meaningful data access for patients or providers.

My contribution focused on the full hardware stack — designing custom wearable ECG hardware (dev kit PCB, final wearable PCB), writing firmware for secure data transmission, and building the integration layer to an AWS-based backend with patient and doctor portals. Billy developed the AWS backend and front-end user interfaces.

Hardware

Three Phases of Hardware Development

Phase 1 — Prototype

Two initial variants: a chest-mount and a clip-on form factor. Used off-the-shelf development boards to validate the ECG signal acquisition concept and test electrode placement strategies.

Phase 2 — Dev Kit PCB

Designed a custom development kit PCB with modular headers for rapid firmware iteration. Included an OLED display for real-time debug output, microSD card logging for data capture, and a 18650 battery holder. This board became the primary platform for iterating on MQTT communication and firmware reliability before committing to the final wearable form factor.

Phase 3 — Final Wearable PCB

The final integrated PCB combined everything into a compact, wearable form factor:

  • AD8232 ECG analog front-end with electrode interface
  • ESP32 microcontroller for processing and WiFi communication
  • MAX1704x fuel gauge for accurate battery state monitoring
  • TP4056 safe Li-ion charging circuitry
  • Test points for manufacturing validation
  • Compact SMD assembly designed for manufacturability

All boards were designed in KiCad with careful attention to footprint selection, DFM guidelines, and in-house assembly. Test points were strategically placed for post-assembly validation.

Firmware & Cloud

End-to-End Data Pipeline

Firmware (ESP32, C++)

The embedded firmware handles real-time ECG signal sampling from the AD8232 front-end, applies basic filtering, and packages the data into encrypted MQTT messages for transmission to the cloud. The Dev Kit PCB served as the primary rapid iteration platform before porting the validated firmware to the final wearable hardware.

Cloud Architecture

Data flows from the wearable device via TLS-encrypted MQTT to AWS IoT Core for secure ingestion and storage. From there, two web portals provide access:

  • Patient portal — personal vitals dashboard, medication tracking, doctor assignments, and device status monitoring
  • Doctor portal — multi-patient monitoring dashboard with real-time ECG viewing, historical data analysis, and AI-powered analytics for detecting health trends

Entrepreneurship

Startup Context & Venture Competition

Berta Medical was co-founded with Billy Zhang as a genuine product development effort, not just a class project. We developed a full revenue model targeting three customer segments: consumers via monthly subscription for device access, small medical practices through a provider-facing service, and medical research facilities through data analytics partnerships.

The complete prototype system — hardware, cloud infrastructure, and web portals — was demonstrated and pitched to physicists, engineers, and business leaders at the Rowan New Venture Competition, receiving feedback on both technical implementation and business viability.
